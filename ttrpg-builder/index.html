TTRPG Builder

A virtual tabletop built for live performance: the game master's face drives the NPCs in real time.

In developmentUnreal Engine 5C++Face tracking

TTRPG Builder is a fantasy tabletop in Unreal Engine 5 with a fixed isometric camera. What sets it apart is performance: a game master or performer opens a small companion web page, their face is tracked right there in the browser, and only the expression data is streamed to the table, where it animates whichever NPC they are voicing. Players join as viewers with a session code or link.

Highlights

  • Live face performance: 52 standard facial expression channels drive the character's face, with mappings for rigs that aren't human-shaped, so orcs, lizardfolk and beasts can perform too.
  • Private tracking: video never leaves the performer's browser; only expression weights cross the network.
  • Lots of races: data-driven character races, from humans and elves to aarakocra, bugbears and dragonborn.
  • Built for mods: content is described by data rather than hard-coded, aiming at mod support and Steam Workshop.

Planned

Rules based on the D&D 5.2 SRD, an optional VR mode, and mod support.
