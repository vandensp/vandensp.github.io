TTRPG Builder

A virtual tabletop built for live performance: the game master's face drives the NPCs in real time.

In developmentUnreal Engine 5C++Face tracking

TTRPG Builder is a fantasy tabletop in Unreal Engine 5 with a fixed isometric camera. What sets it apart is performance: a game master or performer opens a small companion web page, their face is tracked right there in the browser, and only the expression data is streamed to the table, where it animates whichever NPC they are voicing. Players join as viewers with a session code or link.

Highlights

  • Live face performance: 52 standard facial expression channels drive the character's face, with mappings for rigs that aren't human-shaped, so orcs, lizardfolk and beasts can perform too.
  • Private tracking: video never leaves the performer's browser; only expression weights cross the network.
  • Lots of races: data-driven character races, from humans and elves to aarakocra, bugbears and dragonborn.
  • Built for mods: content is described by data rather than hard-coded, aiming at mod support and Steam Workshop.

The table

The maps are built inside the game, not as Unreal levels, so hand-made maps, generated maps and Workshop mods all run through one route that saves as plain data.

  • In-game map builder: paint terrain on a 5 ft grid, raise and lower the ground in visible 5 ft ledges, drag out walls, doors and windows, drop modular building kits, and place tokens — with undo/redo and save.
  • Everything on the 5 ft square: distances, height and movement are counted in D&D 5 ft steps, with difficult terrain, water, climbing and flying built into the ruler and movement ranges.
  • Cutaway interiors: zoom into a building and its roof and upper storeys lift away while walls in front drop to knee height, so you play inside without ever loading a separate map.
  • One camera rig: a single rig glides from an isometric battle view down to an eye-level NPC close-up in one motion of the wheel.
  • World map and travel: a hex world map with full D&D 2024 travel — routes in miles and days, pace, navigation checks and random encounters that drop the party onto a freshly generated battle map, all written to a travel log.
  • 12 seeded biomes: plains, forest, desert, swamp, coast, ruins, town and more generate deterministically from a seed and can then be edited by hand.

Planned

Rules based on the D&D 5.2 SRD, fog of war and per-player vision, an optional VR mode, and mod support.
