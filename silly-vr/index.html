Silly VR

AI companions you can talk to out loud, on your desktop, on a video call, or life-size in VR.

PythonVRLocal AIVoice

Silly VR gives chat characters their own voices and 3D (VRM) bodies. They walk along the bottom of your screen and sit on your windows, join you in a grid like a video call, or stand life-size in a VR room where you can touch them and hand them things. They can look at your screen when asked, use tools on your PC, and keep their own memories, moods and feelings about you and each other.

Highlights

  • Three places, one cast: desktop, video call and VR room share the same characters and memories.
  • Voices of their own: each voice is cloned locally from a short clip, with takes per mood, laughs and whispers, and every line checked for quality before you hear it.
  • Faces and bodies: around 35 expressions built from each avatar's own face shapes, plus gestures and interactions between characters.
  • Lives of their own: they carry on with their own routines when you aren't looking.
  • Phone page: a private web page on your own network for talking to them away from the PC.

Private by design

Everything runs on your own PC. No cloud service is needed, and no characters, avatars or voices are included: you bring your own and follow their licenses.
