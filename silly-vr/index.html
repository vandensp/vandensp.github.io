Silly VR

AI companions you can talk to out loud, on your desktop, on a video call, or life-size in VR.

PythonVRLocal AIVoice

Silly VR gives chat characters their own voices and 3D (VRM) bodies. They walk along the bottom of your screen and sit on your windows, join you in a grid like a video call, or stand life-size in a VR room where you can touch them and hand them things. They can look at your screen when asked, use tools on your PC, and keep their own memories, moods and feelings about you and each other.

Highlights

  • Three places, one cast: desktop, video call and VR room share the same characters and memories. In VR they stand life-size, you can touch them and hand them things, and your controllers buzz with whatever your hands meet.
  • Voices of their own: each voice is cloned locally from one short clip, with a take per mood, laughs and sighs made in her own voice, real whispers, and every line checked for pitch, speaker and crackle before you hear it.
  • Faces and bodies: around 35 expressions built from each avatar's own face shapes, plus gestures, hugs and things two of them do together.
  • Memories and feelings: each one keeps her own notebook, notes on each person and core memories for months, and her fondness, trust and respect toward you and the others drift slowly over weeks with what actually happens.
  • Secrets with a cost: whisper to one character and she whispers back; what's told in confidence she can keep or spill, and spilling has a price. Any of them can mute you or each other for a while.
  • Lives offstage: two characters who aren't in the room may talk in private while the PC is quiet — you can listen in, at a risk — and they miss you, walk out when they've had enough, and notice when you come back.
  • An overseer: one character can be given the whole true picture of the group and the PC, passing on system alerts and holding the others to what's real.
  • Does things on your PC: a helper carries out longer jobs step by step — running fully local, or on your own Claude — and anything that changes something waits for your Approve or Deny. A small planner picks the tool, and a reply that claims a look or read that never happened is caught and redone.
  • Phone page: a private web page on your own network for talking to them away from the PC.

Private by design

Everything runs on your own PC. No cloud service is needed, and no characters, avatars or voices are included: you bring your own and follow their licenses.
