Scary Magic System

A first-person horror game where you cast spells by speaking them or drawing them.

In developmentUnreal Engine 5C++Voice input

You fight with magic you say out loud (phrases like "big frost explosion") or draw as glyphs on a scratchpad you carry. Spells combine an element with a form, and they change the world around you.

The magic

  • Elements: fire, frost, water, air, earth, lightning, nature, arcane, necromancy and life.
  • Forms: bolt, nova, wall, beam, shield and ward, plus great forms like storm, tornado, wave, growth and strike.
  • A world that reacts: fire spreads over magical growth, water douses flames and conducts lightning, frost freezes, wind fans fires, nature entangles.
  • Corruption: casting pulls you around an element wheel, bringing boons, curses, wild magic and hallucinations.

The threat

Elemental zombies (one per element), a stalker that hunts you, and patrolling packs, in a haunted village and elemental levels. You play as Sparrow, carrying a lantern, and can sprint, crouch, vault and glide.
