FACE-INT

Scout any CS2 team on FACEIT and get a game plan against them.

In developmentPythonCS2FACEIT

FACE-INT reads a team's recent FACEIT matches and match demos, builds profiles of how they play, and suggests counter-strategies. Every suggestion comes with the numbers behind it, so you can judge how far to trust it.

What it looks at

  • Map pool: games and win rate per map, with veto suggestions.
  • Economy: eco, light, force, half and full buys per side, and what the team does after pistol rounds.
  • Players: K/D, ADR, headshot %, opening duels, weapon use, AWP share and a role guess.
  • Positions: where each player usually sets up early in the round, and where they get kills and die.
  • T side: which site they hit and how fast.
  • Utility: repeated grenade lineups, likely one-way smokes and kills through smoke.

Output

A single scouting report per team: a ranked list of counter-strats up top, then the map, economy, player, position and utility breakdowns that back them up.

Data

Match history and scoreboards come from the FACEIT Data API. Round-by-round detail comes from match demos.
